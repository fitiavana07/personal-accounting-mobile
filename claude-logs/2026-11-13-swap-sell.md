# Swap mode: SELL (asset with a cost basis -> its intermediary instrument)

Branch: `feat/swap-sell`. Naming follows `docs/swap-feature.md` (BUY): **F** = From account, **T** = To
account, **I_F / I_T** their instruments, **II_F** F's intermediary instrument.

## Request

In transaction creation, Swap mode, implement a SELL between:
- F: an account with instrument I_F **and** intermediary instrument II_F;
- T: an account with instrument II_F (the same as F's intermediary) and **no** intermediary.

Follow-up requirements given during planning:
- Selling back into the intermediary instrument converts F exactly to its proportion of intermediary
  balance when there is no gain/loss. The difference between the typed To amount and that proportional
  cost basis is a gain or loss.
- Depending on the sign, show a spinner of gain accounts (gain) or loss accounts (loss). The amount is
  computed automatically and posted in base currency only (gain/loss accounts are always base-only).
- Add the optional swap fee, deducted from the To amount like in BUY. No secondary fee here.

## Accounting decisions

- Released base `B` and cost basis `C` are F's balances in proportion to the amount sold, as Transfer
  does for the base. If T receives exactly `C`, there is no gain/loss.
- `D = toAmount - C` in II_F units. Its base value is `G = |D| * B / C`: the cost basis' own base rate per
  II_F unit. T therefore carries its II_F at F's cost rate (`B ± G` for `toAmount`), and the transaction
  balances: F credit `B` + gain `G` = T debit `B + G`; for a loss, F credit `B` = T debit `B - G` + loss
  debit `G`.
- The fee is deducted from what T receives; its base value uses the same cost rate (`fee * B / C`) and
  goes to an expense entry. The gain/loss is computed on the **gross** To amount: the fee is a separate
  expense, not a reduction of the proceeds.
- No schema change; saving goes through the normal `saveTransaction` path (stamped by `ChangeTracker`, so
  sync and balance recalculation are unchanged).

## Things worth knowing

- Previously accounts with an intermediary were never swap From accounts; they now appear in From when
  they are funded and a matching To exists. A BUY To that also fits this shape is unaffected.
- The gain/loss account is not saved in templates (like fee amounts it is per transaction); fee expense
  accounts are still stored as before.
- Saving is blocked with the existing "no rate" toast when F has no instrument or no cost basis balance.
- Overdraw uses the existing rule: the amount sold must not exceed F's instrument balance.
- Rounding: each proportional amount is rounded independently (as elsewhere), so `G` may differ by a unit
  from a hand calculation; entries still balance because T's base is derived from the rounded `B` and `G`.
- A gain/loss that rounds to 0 Ar creates no gain/loss entry, even if `D != 0` (the section still shows).
  Saving then still requires choosing an account; acceptable edge case, not optimized.
- No adb/device install was done.

## Manual testing

Setup: an instrument II (e.g. USDT, 2 dp), an instrument I (e.g. BTC, 8 dp), a USDT asset with no
intermediary ("USDT Wallet"), a BTC asset with intermediary USDT ("BTC"), a gain account, a loss account
and an expense account. Fund BTC through a BUY swap (e.g. buy 0.00001 BTC with 1,000 USDT worth 4,500,000 Ar,
so BTC holds 0.00001 BTC, cost basis 1,000 USDT).

1. New transaction > Swap. From shows BTC; To then offers only USDT Wallet (not BTC, not accounts with an
   intermediary).
2. Sell at cost: From 0.000004 BTC (40% of the position), To 400 USDT. No gain/loss section; the From new
   balance shows 0.000006 BTC, 600 USDT, 2,700,000 Ar; To increases by 400 USDT and 1,800,000 Ar.
   Save; balances match, Balance Sheet balances.
3. Gain: To 500 USDT. The section shows "Gain ≈ Ar ..." and a spinner of gain accounts only. Saving without
   a choice shows the gain-account toast. Choose one, save; the gain account is credited that Ar amount in
   base only, USDT Wallet gets 500 USDT and 400/40% of cost + gain in Ar.
4. Loss: To 300 USDT. "Loss ≈ Ar ...", loss accounts only, required; the loss account is debited. Check the
   Income Statement.
5. Type 500 then change to 300: the spinner is reset and now offers loss accounts. Type exactly 400: the
   section disappears.
6. Fee: turn on "Deduct a swap fee", enter 10 USDT and an expense account. "To really gets" line shows the
   net; saved T gets 10 USDT less, the expense account is debited the fee's Ar value (fee * cost rate); the
   gain/loss amount does not change with the fee.
7. Invalid fee (>= To amount) shows the invalid-fee toast. No secondary fee switch is shown.
8. Overdraw: From amount above the BTC balance shows the insufficient-balance toast and the From new balance in red.
9. Switch From to a base-currency account and to an instrument account without intermediary: the usual BUY
   and base swaps still work, and the gain/loss section disappears.
10. Templates: save a template with F = BTC, T = USDT Wallet and apply it: both accounts are preselected.
11. Sync/backup: nothing new is stored (no schema change), existing behaviour applies.
